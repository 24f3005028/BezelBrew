BezelBrew: connect your mail and phone
====================================
Open Settings and choose Gmail, Phone, or Discord in the sidebar.
In Phone, select Android or iPhone to see the corresponding setup.
Setup starts off. Nothing connects to an account just by opening a page.

GMAIL
1. Choose Sign in with Google.
2. Pick your account in your browser and approve Inbox previews.
3. Your latest subjects, senders and unread count appear in the envelope tab.

Your password stays with Google. BezelBrew cannot send, delete, or read the body
of your messages. Choose Disconnect to stop previews and remove local access.
You can also remove the app's grant at https://myaccount.google.com/connections .

If Sign in is unavailable and the page says the publisher needs to set it up,
this build has not been configured for Google yet. You do not need a developer
account or a credentials file. Open Gmail still works in your browser.

FOR THE APP PUBLISHER: Advanced settings retains personal-client imports.
The separate GooglePublisherSetup.txt explains how to configure Google once
when building the app, so end users can simply sign in.

ANDROID
1. Install the free KDE Connect app on this PC and your Android phone. The
   setup page has buttons for Microsoft Store and Google Play.
2. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi. Open KDE Connect on both and pair them.
   On the phone, select this PC, turn on Notification sync, and allow Android's
   notification access permission when requested.
3. Return to BezelBrew and choose Find my phone. Pick your phone by name and
   choose Connect this phone. Your choice is saved automatically.

BezelBrew searches standard install locations and running KDE Connect apps.
You normally do not need an executable path or device ID. If more than one
paired device appears, choose the correct one; BezelBrew will not pick for you.

If your phone is missing, keep KDE Connect open, check Wi-Fi and pairing, then
try Find my phone again. If the phone connects but alerts do not arrive, choose
Notifications missing? for permission instructions. Some apps can hide content.
The companion app can also need an update. Advanced settings retains manual
paths, device IDs and refresh intervals for unusual installations.

Disconnect stops BezelBrew sync; your existing KDE Connect pairing is kept.
Phone notifications are read-only and kept in memory. Replying and remote
dismissal are not implemented. KDE Connect is a separate open-source program;
its background resource use is separate from BezelBrew's.

IPHONE (BETA)
1. Choose Bluetooth settings and pair your iPhone with this PC.
2. On iPhone: Settings > Bluetooth > this PC. Turn on Share System Notifications
   if that option is available.
3. Choose Check connection. New authorized alerts can appear in the phone tab.

This feature depends on Windows, iOS and your Bluetooth driver. A paired phone
does not always offer notifications to Windows. Hardware validation is still
pending; the connection status reports when sharing is unavailable.

Use Phone Link instead opens Microsoft's separate app and its own setup flow.
BezelBrew does not import Phone Link's private notification feed.

DISCORD
Open the Discord desktop window and join a voice channel. In BezelBrew, choose
Connectors > Discord > Toggle Discord mute. BezelBrew switches to Discord and
sends Ctrl+Shift+M. Your current channel and mute state are visible in Discord.

Privacy and background use
Account sign-in, notification permissions and phone pairing are approved by you.
There is no BezelBrew cloud account or notification relay. User mail tokens use
Windows Credential Manager. Headers and notifications are not saved to the INI.
Connectors are off by default. Finding a phone runs only when you request it;
enabled sync checks at its saved interval. Pause also suspends connector work.
